1.9 · Internet

The Internet

DefinitionThe Internet is the largest computer network. It is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the standard protocol TCP/IP.

  • It links numerous private, public, academic, business and government networks. Computers connected over the Internet can send and receive text, graphics, voice, video and programs.
  • It is formed by joining thousands of smaller networks such as LAN, MAN and WAN, so it is also called the network of networks.
  • Telephone links, satellites, microwaves, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and other technologies connect millions of computers.
  • No one owns the Internet, although many organizations collaborate on its functioning. The high-speed fiber-optic backbones are owned by telephone companies in their countries.
1.9 · Internet

Benefits of the Internet

Information

Easy access to information on any topic.

Communication

Email, chat and video calls.

Online education

Online classes, e-learning and research.

Entertainment

Movies, music, games and social media.

Online services

Banking, shopping, ticket booking and bill payment.

Global connection

Connects people all over the world.

Business and jobs

Online business, freelancing and job searching.

1.9 · Internet

Limitations of the Internet

Security risks

Viruses, hacking and online fraud.

Privacy problems

Personal information may be misused.

Addiction

Overuse wastes time and affects health.

Cyberbullying

Online harassment.

False information

Not all information is correct.

Health issues

Eye strain and stress from long screen time.

Dependence

Internet problems can stop work or study.

1.9 · Child Safety

Online child protection

DefinitionOnline child protection means keeping children safe while using the Internet. The Internet is useful for learning and communication, but it also has risks.

Why it matters

Children use the Internet for study, games and social media. Some people misuse it to harm or cheat children. Children may face cyberbullying, fake messages or unsafe content.

Online risks

Cyberbullying. Online strangers. Inappropriate content. Online scams. Loss of privacy.

1.9 · Child Safety

Ways to stay safe online

Do not share

Never share address, phone number, school name or passwords.

Strong passwords

Create passwords that are hard to guess.

Careful with strangers

Do not chat or meet unknown people online.

Privacy settings

Keep social media accounts private.

Think before clicking

Avoid unknown links, ads or downloads.

Tell a trusted adult

Report any uncomfortable or scary online experience.

1.9 · Child Safety

Parents, teachers and reporting abuse

Role of parents and teachers

Guide children on safe Internet use. Monitor online activities. Teach digital manners and ethics.

Reporting online abuse

Call 1098, the Child Helpline, for help and protection. Report online abuse through CWIN Nepal and trusted authorities.

1.9 · Intranet

Intranet

DefinitionAn intranet is a private network used inside an organization, such as a school, office, bank or company. It works like the Internet but is accessible only to authorized people. It uses the same protocol as the Internet.

  • Used within an organization only. Access is restricted and secure.
  • Helps in sharing information and resources. Uses web browsers and email.
  • Improves communication and teamwork. Faster and safer than the public Internet.
1.9 · Intranet

Intranet: uses, advantages and limitations

Uses

Sharing notices, documents and files. Internal emails and messages. Online attendance and staff records. Internal websites and databases. Communication between teachers, students and staff.

Advantages

High security and privacy. Saves time and paper. Improves efficiency. Easy access to internal information.

Limitations

Limited access, cannot be used easily outside. Needs maintenance and technical support. Setup cost can be high.

1.9 · Extranet

Extranet

DefinitionAn extranet is a private network that allows limited access to external users such as customers, suppliers or business partners. It is an extension of an organization intranet, but access is given only to authorized outsiders.

  • Used by both internal and external users. Access is restricted and secure.
  • Requires a login ID and password. Uses web browsers and other Internet technologies.
  • Helps in safe information sharing.
1.9 · Extranet

Extranet: uses, advantages and limitations

Uses

Sharing information with business partners. Online order processing and tracking. Sharing project details with clients. Collaboration between organizations.

Advantages

Improves business communication. Saves time and cost. Controlled access to information. Improves efficiency and coordination.

Limitations

Security risks if not managed properly. Setup and maintenance costs are high. Requires an Internet connection.
