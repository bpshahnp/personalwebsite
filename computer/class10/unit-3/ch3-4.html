3.4 · Waveform

Introduction to audio waveform

DefinitionAn audio waveform is a visual representation of sound. It shows how the sound wave changes over time.

  • The horizontal line (x-axis) represents time.
  • The vertical line (y-axis) represents the loudness or amplitude of the sound.

3.4 · Waveform

Why waveforms are needed

  • They help in editing audio (cutting, adjusting volume).
  • Used in music production, podcasts and sound effects.
  • Visualizing sound makes it easier to work with.
  • Example: audio editing software like Audacity or GarageBand shows sound as waveforms, making it easier to cut, mix or adjust the audio.
3.4 · Formats

Audio file formats

Audio file formats save and play sound recordings. The most used are MP3, AAC and WAV.

MP3

MPEG Audio Layer 3. Compresses audio to reduce size without greatly affecting quality. Takes less space, easy to download and share. Plays on almost any device.

AAC

Advanced Audio Coding. Higher quality than MP3 at the same file size. Default for YouTube, Apple devices and many smartphones.

WAV

Waveform Audio File Format. Stores sound without compression, so quality is high but files are larger. Used in studios and professional audio.
