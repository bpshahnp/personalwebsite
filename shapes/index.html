1 grid square = 1 unit. Polygon: click points, then double-click, Enter, or click the first point. Drag handles to reshape. Delete removes, Esc cancels. Drag on empty canvas to select several shapes (Shift adds), then drag to move them. Pan with the ✋ tool, Space+drag, middle mouse or the scroll wheel; zoom with Ctrl+wheel or the buttons.
