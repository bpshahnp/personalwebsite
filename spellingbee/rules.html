Competition Rules

1

Each team gets its turn according to the competition schedule.

2

The moderator presents or plays the word before the contestant spells it.

3

The contestant must enter or spell the word exactly as heard.

4

A correct spelling earns the points assigned to that question.

5

A team may pass when the moderator allows it.

6

The moderator can end a turn when the answer is incorrect or time expires.

7

The official answer is revealed only after the turn is completed.

8

Questions and points are controlled from the admin dashboard.

9

Scores are updated online and displayed on the live scoreboard.

10

The moderator’s decision is final for the competition session.
