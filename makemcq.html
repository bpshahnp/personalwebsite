MCQ Paper Maker

Columns apply when 1 copy per sheet is selected. With 2 or 4 copies, each copy is a single column.
Auto-save is on.
Print and Export PDF both open your browser’s print dialog. For printing, pick your printer; for PDF, choose “Save as PDF”. Use scale 100% and turn off headers and footers. The paper size and orientation are already set from your choices.
Import raw questions
Paste questions, or load a .txt file. Numbering is optional. Options can be A) / (a) / a. / A: on separate lines or in one line. Optionally add a line like “Layout: 2x2” (or 1x4, 4x1, auto) after a question. Mark the answer with a line like “Answer: B” or put * before or after the correct option.
